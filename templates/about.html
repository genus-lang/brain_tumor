Academic & Research Initiative

About NeuroScan AI • Deep Learning Architecture

NeuroScan AI investigates the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and explainability techniques in computer-aided neurological imaging, bridging the gap between deep feature extraction and interpretable clinical saliency.

VGG16 Transfer Learning Pipeline

16-LAYER CNN

Model Formulation

The core classification engine utilizes the VGG16 architecture pre-trained on ImageNet. To adapt general visual features to complex neurological MRI textures, a staged transfer learning approach was employed:

  • Feature Extraction Backbone (Blocks 1–4): Frozen convolutional layers capturing foundational edge, gradient, and texture representations.
  • Domain Adaptation (Block 5): Fine-tuned with a low learning rate (\(1 \times 10^{-4}\)) on MRI brain parenchyma to learn tumor boundaries and tissue densities.
  • Classification Head: Custom top layers incorporating Global Flattening, Batch Normalization, Dropout (0.3 and 0.2), Dense (256 units), and a 4-way Softmax activation.

Dataset Diagnostic Pathology Classes

7,200 SCANS

Glioma

Intra-axial glial support cell neoplasms with diffuse infiltration into brain parenchyma. Common grades: astrocytoma, oligodendroglioma, glioblastoma multiforme.

Meningioma

Arachnoid cap cell origin from the meninges. Predominantly slow-growing, extra-axial lesions causing focal compression on adjacent cortex and cranial nerves.

Pituitary Adenoma

Sella turcica lesions originating in the anterior pituitary gland, frequently presenting with hyperprolactinemia, Cushingoid changes, or optic chiasm compression.

No Tumor (Normal)

Physiological anatomical contours displaying symmetric lateral and third ventricles, intact midline alignment, and absence of mass effect or peritumoral vasogenic edema.

Academic Scope & Technical Limitations

This system evaluates single 2D axial MRI slices and does not incorporate multi-parametric 3D volumetric DICOM acquisitions, perfusion-weighted imaging (PWI), or MR spectroscopy. It is designed solely for computational imaging research and educational demonstrations.
