Supported Classification Categories

The deep neural network evaluates features against the four pathological classes defined in the clinical training dataset.

Glioma

Intra-axial glial cell tumor exhibiting diffuse infiltration into surrounding brain parenchyma.

Meningioma

Extra-axial neoplasm originating from arachnoid cells, exerting mass effect on adjacent neural structures.

Pituitary Tumor

Sella turcica adenoma frequently associated with endocrine alterations or optic chiasm displacement.

No Tumor

Physiological anatomical contours without radiological evidence of space-occupying lesions or edema.
